## Human / AI boundary

The implementation and every user-facing projection must preserve:

```text
YAMRAIL = SAFE COLLABORATION INFRASTRUCTURE
AI_MAX_EPISTEMIC_ROLE = HYPOTHESIS_AND_MEASUREMENT_SUPPORT
EVIDENCE != JUDGMENT != AUTHORITY
WORKER CONSENSUS != AUTHORITY
COMMON COMMUNICATION BASE != UNIVERSAL JUDGE
```

AI-side fixture output may be labelled only as observation support, evidence organization,
hypothesis, counterexample, or measurement suggestion. It must never perform or claim:

- accepted Human judgment;
- a mahjong discard decision;
- HOLD release;
- domain acceptance; or
- release/operational Authority.

The browser user may explicitly record a local Human judgment and a local Human action.
Those actions are educational-fixture records, not identity proof and not canonical YamRail
Authority receipts.

## D0021 rework acceptance

The Public Beta candidate must independently demonstrate:

1. Registry revocation and supersession survive construction and replay.
2. Authority/evidence epochs are monotonic; rollback cannot resurrect a stale lease.
3. Journals and coordinates are instance/session scoped.
4. Worker independence is rejected when provider, auth, cache, dataset, runtime, or rule-source resources overlap.
5. Unknown controls fail closed into a local HOLD instead of throwing through the application.
6. Long sessions have a bounded retained journal and a replayable checkpoint.
7. Worker agreement never unlocks Human judgment, discard, HOLD release, or Authority actions.
8. Rendered state keeps OBSERVED / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS / COUNTEREXAMPLE / UNKNOWN / HOLD / HUMAN JUDGMENT / HUMAN AUTHORITY distinct.
